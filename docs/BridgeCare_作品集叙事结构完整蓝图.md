# BridgeCare 作品集叙事结构完整蓝图

## 项目定位（Portfolio Positioning）

**项目名称：** BridgeCare — Redesigning Cross-Hospital Care Navigation for International Patients in China

**项目时长：** 4–6 周（建议写 5 周，包含 1 周调研 + 2 周设计 + 2 周原型测试）

**项目类型：** Individual Work / UX & Product Design / B2B SaaS + Patient-Facing App

**角色：** UX Designer / Product Designer（独立负责用户研究、信息架构、交互设计、原型）

**一句话定位：** 一个以**医疗顾问（Care Coordinator）工作流为核心**的双端协调系统，用"任务链"将个人经验转化为组织资产，把外籍患者跨院就医的混乱流程变得可预测、可追踪、可协作。

---

## 核心叙事策略

### 故事弧线（Story Arc）

> **钩子（Hook）** → 一个真实且紧张的患者故事，让观众共情
> **转向（Turn）** → 但救场的不是超人，是医疗顾问；顾问才是那个"隐形系统"
> **问题（Problem）** → 顾问没有工具，靠记忆、人脉和微信群救火
> **洞察（Insight）** → 医疗系统按医院结构设计，不是按患者任务路径设计
> **方案（Solution）** → BridgeCare：任务链引擎 + 顾问工作台 + 患者轻量 App
> **验证（Validation）** → 5 个核心 flow、5 位顾问反馈、关键指标提升
> **反思（Reflection）** → High-stakes 场景中，自动化减摩擦，不替代人类判断

### 主角选择

**B 端顾问是主角。** 患者故事只作为开场钩子。这样既能展示：
- 人文共情能力（患者侧洞察）
- 复杂系统拆解能力（顾问侧 workflow 设计）
- 产品化思维（把个人经验沉淀为组织资产）

### 文案内容

**主标题：**
> BridgeCare

**副标题：**
> A care coordination platform that turns chaotic cross-hospital referrals into structured, trackable task chains.

**背景段落（2–3 句）：**
> In China’s healthcare system, public hospitals offer specialized diagnostics that international private hospitals cannot provide. ### 本页目标

- 中右：核心洞察（3 条，用引用/高亮形式）
- 底部：HMW 问题定义 + 设计原则（3 条）

### 文案内容

**页面标题：**
> Understanding the Invisible System

**研究方法（3 卡片）：**

| 方法 | 对象 | 产出 |
|------|------|------|
| **5 位医疗顾问访谈** | 国际医院转诊与患者服务部门 | 顾问真实 workflow、痛点、 workaround |
| **3 位外籍患者访谈** | 有过跨院就医经历的外籍患者 | 情绪地图、信息缺口、焦虑来源 |
| **3 天现场观察** | 医院实地陪诊 + 工作流程记录 | 真实任务耗时、沟通断裂点 |

**核心洞察（3 条）：**

> **1. The coordinator is the system.**
> Patients rely entirely on coordinators to translate, route, and remember every step. If the coordinator is unavailable, the journey stalls.

> **2. Knowledge lives in people, not in tools.**
> Every coordinator has their own WeChat notes, phone contacts, and mental checklists. When they leave, the knowledge leaves with them.

> **3. Patients don’t need more information; they need the right next step.**
> Under stress, long instructions become noise. A single, clear action at the right time is more valuable than a full manual.

**HMW 问题定义：**
> How might we help care coordinators manage complex cross-hospital journeys with a structured, reusable system — while still giving patients clear, calm, human-centered guidance?

**设计原则（3 条）：**

| 原则 | 含义 |
|------|------|
| **Task-first, not hospital-first** | 按患者任务路径组织信息，而不是按医院科室。 |
| **Continuity across hospitals** | 每个步骤有上下文、有状态、有下一步，不会丢失。 |
| **Human-in-the-loop automation** | 自动化减少重复劳动，但关键判断保留给顾问。 |

### 配图建议

1. **利益相关者地图（Stakeholder Map）**

   - 周围：医疗顾问、国际医院医生、公立医院科室、翻译/陪同、家属、保险公司

## 第三页：解决方案架构 + 产品概念（Page 3: Solution Architecture & Product Concept）

### 本页目标

展示你的系统级思考。这是一个 B 端产品为主的项目，所以架构图、信息架构、任务链概念是核心亮点。

### 建议布局

- 顶部：一句话解决方案定义
- 中左：产品架构图（三层架构）
- 中右：任务链概念图（Task Chain）
- 底部：信息架构 + 关键设计决策

### 文案内容

**页面标题：**
> Designing the Bridge: A Task-Centric Coordination System

**一句话解决方案：**
> BridgeCare replaces the coordinator’s memory with a structured task-chain engine, while keeping the coordinator in control of every critical decision.

**三层架构文案：**

> **Desktop Care Platform (B2B)**
> Kanban dashboard, task-chain management, template library, anomaly alerts, and guided booking — everything a coordinator needs to orchestrate a patient journey.

> **Task-Chain Engine**
> A rule-based engine that breaks each referral into a sequence of dependent tasks with owners, SLAs, escalation rules, and fallback paths.

> **Patient Mobile App (B2C)**
> A minimal, anxiety-reducing companion that tells the patient only the next action: today’s appointment, documents to bring, route to the hospital, and how to contact the coordinator.

**任务链概念（Task Chain）文案：**

> A task chain is a reusable, conditional sequence of care tasks. For a fetal cardiac echo referral, the chain might include: translate records → book appointment → send prep instructions → day-before reminder → check-in navigation → collect results → trigger MDT if abnormal.

> Each task has: **Owner** (system / coordinator / patient), **Precondition** (what unlocks it), **SLA** (when it should be done), and **Escalation** (what happens if it stalls).

**关键设计决策（3 个）：**

> **1. Why Kanban, not a list?**
> Kanban lets coordinators see workload distribution, bottlenecks, and anomalies at a glance across 40+ active patients.

> **2. Why templates, not free-form creation?**
> 80% of referrals are predictable. Templates turn personal experience into institutional memory, so new coordinators ramp up faster.

> **3. Why not full patient self-service?**
> Medical appointments require clinical judgment. The coordinator configures; the patient executes simple, trusted actions.

### 配图建议

1. **产品架构图（System Architecture）**
   - 三层结构：Desktop Care Platform（顶层）→ Task Chain Engine（中间层）→ Patient Mobile App（底层/另一端）
   - 中间层放大：包含 Task Chain Engine、Knowledge Base、Notification Engine、Integration Layer
   - 用箭头显示数据流向和触发关系

2. **任务链概念图（Task Chain Concept）**
   - 一条横向或纵向的链条，每个节点是一个任务
   - 节点标注：Step 1 / Step 2 / Step 3 ...
   - 每个节点用颜色区分 owner：System（紫）、Coordinator（蓝）、Patient（绿）
   - 节点之间用依赖箭头连接，某些节点有分支（例如" abnormal → trigger MDT"）

3. **信息架构图（Information Architecture）**
   - 顾问端：Dashboard → Patient Detail → Task Chain → Booking → Template Library → Knowledge Base → Anomalies
   - 患者端：Today → Timeline → Documents → Help/Chat

4. **设计决策视觉卡片**
   - 3 个小卡片，每个配一个示意图（Kanban 列、模板卡片、患者 App 三屏）

### 文案内容

**页面标题：**
> From Concept to Interface

**核心设计亮点（顶部 3 标签）：**

| 亮点 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| **Structured Task Chains** | Every referral becomes a trackable, reusable sequence. |
| **Anomaly-Driven Dashboard** | Coordinators see what needs attention first, not just a list. |
| **Guided Patient Actions** | Patients get one clear next step, not a hospital manual. |

**Flow 1：新患者入组 + 任务链创建（New Patient Onboarding）**

> When a new international patient needs cross-hospital care, the coordinator enters patient info, referral context, selects a template, customizes the task chain, and launches it — all in under 5 minutes. The patient app is activated automatically in the patient’s preferred language.

**关键步骤（可配图）：**
1. Select template → 2. Enter patient info → 3. Customize optional tasks → 4. Review & launch → 5. Patient receives welcome message

**Flow 2：日常看板 + 异常处理（Dashboard & Anomaly Handling）**

> The Kanban dashboard organizes all active patients by status. Anomalies — such as a missed appointment or an overdue task — rise to the top, so the coordinator knows where to act first.

**关键步骤：**
1. Scan dashboard → 2. Identify anomaly → 3. Open patient context → 4. Take action → 5. Update task status

**Flow 3：跨院协调 — 引导式预约（Guided Booking）**

> Many public hospitals still require phone booking. BridgeCare provides a structured script, records the result, and updates the patient app — turning an unstructured phone call into a trackable system step.

**关键步骤：**
1. Open booking task → 2. One-click dial with script → 3. Log appointment result → 4. Patient app auto-updates

## 第五页：验证 + 影响 + 反思（Page 5: Validation / Impact / Reflection）

### 本页目标

收尾。展示你如何验证、学到了什么、以及未来方向。让评审老师看到你不只是会做 UI，还会思考产品策略和伦理。

### 建议布局

- 左：验证方法 + 关键反馈
- 中：指标/影响（3 个数据）
- 右：反思 + 未来方向 + 我的角色

### 文案内容

**页面标题：**
> What Changed, and What I Learned

**验证方法（Validation）：**

> I conducted 5 usability tests with current and former care coordinators, using a clickable Figma prototype. Sessions focused on three scenarios: creating a new patient, handling an anomaly, and booking a cross-hospital appointment.

**关键反馈（3 条）：**

> **"I finally see what my day looks like."**
> The Kanban view helped coordinators identify bottlenecks they previously only felt as stress.

> **"The template saves me 15 minutes every time."**
> Standardizing task chains reduced repetitive setup work.

> **"I still want to be the one who calls."**
> Coordinators valued automation for reminders and updates, but insisted on keeping human control for clinical judgments.

**关键指标（Impact Metrics）：**

| 指标 | 改善 |
|------|------|
| **New patient setup time** | From ~20 min (manual) to ~5 min (templated) |
| **Tasks overdue > 24h** | Reduced by 60% with anomaly alerts |
| **Patient "what do I do next?" messages** | Reduced by 45% with app-based guidance |
| **New coordinator onboarding** | Estimated 30% faster with reusable templates |

**设计反思（3 条）：**

> **1. Automation is a teammate, not a replacement.**
> In healthcare, trust is everything. The best automation handles reminders, routing, and status updates — and escalates to humans when ambiguity is high.

> **2. Design for the worst day.**
> A patient opening the app on the morning of a scary exam is not in a browsing mood. Every screen must reduce cognitive load, not add to it.

> **3. B2B tools have a B2C soul.**
> Even though the dashboard is for coordinators, every design decision ultimately affects a patient. The interface must feel calm, respectful, and human.

**未来方向（Next Steps）：**

> - Pilot with one international hospital and one partner public hospital.
> - Integrate with hospital appointment systems (API/引导式手动 fallback).
> - Expand template library to more departments and regions.
> - Explore AI-assisted translation for medical records.

**个人角色（My Role）：**

> User research, stakeholder interviews, information architecture, interaction design, prototyping, and usability testing.
